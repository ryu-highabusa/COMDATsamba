RYU HAYABUSA

RYU came to the states as a scholarship student, and still considers his move here as temporary.

Even as a teenager he was unusually serious about life--he became a Ninja master at his doujou when he was only 16.

Now 23, Ryu teaches a ninjitsu style called "Hayabusa Ninjitsu" at his own place --a combination antique shop/doujou on Vine Street in Downtown Cincinnati.

RYU HAYABUSA

HAYABUSA NINJITSU Style LETHAL FIGHTER


His physical strength comes from years of serious training as a rock climber back in his hometown of Ashikaga, Tochigi.

Lunchtime on these hikes always consists of fresh sushi straight out of the mountain streams, caught with Hayabusa's bare-hands.

A true perfectionist, what this 5'9"154# Ninja lacks in brawn, he makes up for in speed and careful logic.
