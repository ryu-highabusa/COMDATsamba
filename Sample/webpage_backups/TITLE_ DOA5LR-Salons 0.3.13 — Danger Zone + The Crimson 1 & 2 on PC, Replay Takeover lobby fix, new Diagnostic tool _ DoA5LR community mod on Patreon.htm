TITLE: DOA5LR-Salons 0.3.13 — Danger Zone + The Crimson 1 & 2 on PC, Replay Takeover lobby fix, new Diagnostic tool

4 days ago

Hi everyone! Big update for the DOA5LR community mod pack: three PS4 stages are now playable on PC, offline AND online, the Replay Takeover crash in lobbies is fixed, and there's a new tool to send us your logs in one click when something goes wrong.

DOWNLOAD

https://github.com/FgcSnow/DOA5LR-Salons/releases/latest

Already have the pack? Close the game and run DOA5LR-Salons-Installer.exe (or click "Update now" when it pops up after you close the game). Accept the installer update to 1.3.3 first, then update the pack.

NEW STAGES — DANGER ZONE + THE CRIMSON 1 & 2 (PS4 versions)

Pick them by hand in the stage select, with proper thumbnails. Crimson 1 and 2 share one slot: switch the variant in Solo.

Or tick them in the game's Random filter: three new check boxes (Danger Zone / The Crimson 1 / The Crimson 2).

Offline and online lobbies. All the original stages are still there.

They are an installer check box, "Maps", ON by default. Untick it if you don't want them: their files are removed, your settings are kept. Tick it again to get them back.

⚠️ ONLINE: EVERYONE IN THE ROOM (players AND spectators) NEEDS THE MAPS. Please update before joining rooms that use them. A player without the maps would see another stage and the match would most likely desync.

REPLAY TAKEOVER — LOBBY CRASH FIX (2.6)

After 0.3.11, some players crashed while browsing the lobby list or creating a room. Replay Takeover only works in replays, but version 2.5 kept some of its hooks active everywhere, lobbies included.

Replay Takeover 2.6 goes fully to sleep while you are online (lobby list, rooms, online matches, spectating): the game runs untouched, and F5/F6/F7 are left to the other mods (F7 = copy the room link). Offline replays work exactly as before. It was tested in real lobbies by the player who had the crashes.

Reminder of what it does in a replay: L2 (or F5) takes control of P1 or P2, Select jumps back to that moment and keeps control, Start jumps back and gives control back to the replay, L3 (or F7) jumps back with the stage rebuilt (broken walls, tables and benches come back). Installer check box "Replay Takeover", on by default.

NEW — DOA5LR-DIAGNOSTIC (if the game crashes or freezes)

Open DOA5LR-Diagnostic\DOA5LR Diagnostic.cmd in your game folder:

Check: verifies the stages, the lobby modules (rooms, invites, JoinFix, wired/Wi-Fi, updater), your other mods and your AutoLink folder.

Play (with the probe): starts a small read-only observer (no hook, no injection) that notes the stage, the fight and how the game exits, then launches the game through Steam.

Send my logs: builds a ZIP on your Desktop, SHOWS YOU WHAT'S INSIDE, and sends it to us on Discord ONLY if you confirm. If the game is frozen, click it BEFORE closing the game. The optional .dmp file shows exactly where the game crashed.

Detailed crash reports (optional, admin rights once): Windows keeps a small .dmp when the game crashes. It really helps us fix bugs.

Never sent: IP, SteamID, chat, controller inputs, saves. The .dmp holds a piece of the game's memory and may include your Steam name — untick it if you prefer.

WHICH FOLDERS GO WHERE (please check yours!)

Your game folder (Steam → DOA5LR → Manage → Browse local files) should look like this:

Dead or Alive 5 Last Round

game.exe

dinput8.dll / dinput8Hooked.dll / DInput8.ini ........ loader + AutoLink

DOA5LR-ReplayTakeover.asi / .ini ...................... Replay Takeover

DOA5LR-DangerZone / DNZ- / Crimson / ExtraStages /

RandomStages .asi + .ini .............................. the new stages

CodexDangerZone\ CodexCrimson\ PS4Stages\ ........... stage data (don't touch)

scripts\ ............................................. lobby plugins (Lobby, JoinFix, InviteFix,

WiFi-Wired, UpdateCheck, Borderless, 60 fps) + guides

AutoLink\ ............................................ ONLY character folders (AYANE, KASUMI, NYOTENGU...)

+ Movie\ Texture\ Sound\ Stages

DOA5LR-Diagnostic\ ................................... the new Diagnostic tool

DOA5LR-Salons-Backups\ ............................... backups made by the installer

IMPORTANT — AUTOLINK: costume mods work fine with the new stages, but DO NOT keep modding tools inside AutoLink (LR Photo Studio, TMC tools, Archive Tool, converters...). During the tests, a player crashed after every first fight because of a "_Modding" folder full of tools inside AutoLink; as soon as he moved it out of the game folder, the crashes stopped. Keep your tools somewhere else (for example Documents\DOA5LR-Modding). The Diagnostic tool warns you if it finds an unknown folder in AutoLink.

ANTIVIRUS DELETED A FILE?

The installer now also watches JoinFix (the room join fix) and the main stage modules. If an antivirus removed one, the installer offers to repair the pack when you close the game — lobbies, invites, the wired/Wi-Fi tag and the room fixes are put back exactly as they should be. Everything else in the pack is byte-identical to 0.3.12.

PRIVACY

Nothing is ever sent automatically. The only thing that can leave your PC is the ZIP of "Send my logs", after you've seen its content and confirmed. The stage modules write local logs next to the game (for crash reports); Replay Takeover and Lobby also write a local log. Sources are in the pack. No game content, no Steam/DLC files.

HOW TO UPDATE

Installer already there: close the game → "Update now" (or run DOA5LR-Salons-Installer.exe → UPDATE). Accept the installer update to 1.3.3 first.

Fresh install: download DOA5LR-Salons-Installer.exe from the link above, run it, click INSTALL.

The download is bigger this time (about 270 MB) because it contains the stages.

Pre-release testers (dz-crimson-test r4/r5): just update with the installer; to remove the maps, untick "Maps" in the installer (not the old test window).

Thanks to everyone who tested the stages in lobbies and sent logs tonight — special thanks to nihyru for all the crash tests that led to the AutoLink finding, and to BonuStage for Replay Takeover. Made with ❤️ by FGCsnow & BonuStage for the DOA5LR community. Your support here keeps the project going — thank you!
