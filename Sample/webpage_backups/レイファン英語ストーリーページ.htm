LEI FANG
Lei Fang is an impulsive little vixen, which is actually how she ended up in Missoula, Montana, anyway.

She applied and was accepted to MSU's overseas scholarship program, and before she knew it she found herself in this small Montana mountain-basin town.

Her only regret is that her favorite hobby -karaoke- is an unheard-of pastime up here, where mountain climbing and kayaking are the norm.
