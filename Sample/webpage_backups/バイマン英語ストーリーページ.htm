BAYMAN

A history scholar and aspiring displomat,this 31 year old got his first taste of the seamy side of life after the USSR break-up, and never looked back.

Bayman was trained by the Russian military in commando-style fighting, and served as a special agent until the day he made one, fatal, mistake involving the Kremlin`s daughter:

he was banished from the special forces forever, and exiled to Brighton Beach, New York.

BAYMAN

RUSSINAN COMMANDO Style LETHAL FIGHTER

At 231 pounds and 6`6", Bayman is an unlikely, yet accomplished, chef --beef stew is his specialty.

Now supporting himself as a professional assassin, Bayman lives in a small corner apartment in Downtown New York with his doberman, "Buffy." He spends his free time between hits and DOA matches collecting obscure weapons and playing chess against an E-mail partner he`s never met.
