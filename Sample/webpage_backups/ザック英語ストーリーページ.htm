ZACK

Zack was born in Blissfield, Michigan. (Being from such a remote mid-western town is probably what gave him his primary motivation to study martial arts.)

The 25 years old considers Muetai just a hobby, even though he`s already mastered the Thai-Style Boxing.

A Pacifist, Zack spends one weekend a month volunteering as a "big brother" --and his "little brothers" don`t have a clue about Zack`s DOA activities.

ZACK

MUETAI Style LETHAL FIGHTER (THAI-STYLE BOXING)

This 172 pound lethal fighter doubles as a DJ/bouncer at a Toledo club, where he also holds reign as the city billiards champ.

Zack stands 6` tall and isn`t done eating until he`s had ice cream with strawberries and chocolate.

He`s had his `76 Bronco since way before it was the thing to do, and spends all his spare time between DOA fights tinkering on the thing.
