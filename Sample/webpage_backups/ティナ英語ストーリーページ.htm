TINA
This blue-eyed beauty is no typical American woman--even though she is from Kansas City, loves cycling marathons, won't tell her age or her weight, and collects friends like gnats on a ripe banana.

Tina isn't typical, because she's been a pro wrestler since high school, and is now one of DOA's most feared competitors.

TINA

Tina is no typical American woman: she's a lethal fighter to be reckoned with...
