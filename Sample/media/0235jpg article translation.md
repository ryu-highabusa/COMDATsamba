## Page 1: Introduction & Gameplay Overview

### Main Title

**The first character reveal is "Kasumi." We also bring you the details on the morphing technology that brings realistic breast physics to life.**
*Staff Writer: Addy "Smile" Toshi*

### Main Text

Well, lately I’ve been getting super excited about the re-releases of High Complete Models and thinking about buying a ton of them... wait, you don’t care about my personal life? Right. What really matters is discussing how far along the development of this game, ***Dead or Alive***, actually is.

On the graphics front, new stages and character designs are steadily being completed. The stage shown starting on the next page looks absolutely fantastic, doesn't it?

Let's dive a bit into the combat system. Pressing **H (Hold/Grapple button) + G** executes a standard throw. Down attacks are divided into two types: a high-damage jumping attack executed by pressing ⬆⬆⬆ (varies by character), and a quick, low-damage, low-recovery ground strike performed without jumping. At this stage of development, the game won't register down-attack inputs unless the opponent's back is fully flat on the ground.

Currently, six characters have visible completed graphics. I believe there were only five up until the AOU Show, but a cool, distinguished old martial artist has been added to the roster. The game's engine has the capacity to hold up to eight characters, so we might see one more added down the line.

Additionally, the Muay Thai user who has appeared in this magazine multiple times (named Kelly) is reportedly undergoing a major overhaul. When I played him last time, he felt incredibly strong, but we'll see...

Furthermore, we've discovered that this game's signature feature, the **"Hold" (counter/grapple)** mechanism, works even from a crouching position and is highly effective against low-level attacks. It might have been my own misconception, but I assumed a one-button counter move available to all characters would only work against high and mid-level attacks...

In the next issue, we will take a deep dive into the graphics side of things!

## Page 2: Character Profile — Kasumi

### Headlines

**KASUMI** | **DEAD OR ALIVE**
*Character Feature — Part 1*

### Story

**The Mugen Tenshin Clan Style:** A ninja art of melting into the fog, becoming an illusion to strike down enemies. Since childhood, Kasumi has trained alongside her brother, Hayate, under the strict tutelage of their father and the 17th clan leader, Shiden. Her mastery of taijutsu (martial arts) has made her an unrivaled fighter.

The incident occurred on the very day Shiden intended to pass the leadership over to Hayate. Hayate never returned from his training mission. A month later, he was found covered in blood, brutally injured and paralyzed.

*"Kasumi, you must succeed me."*

On the night before her 18th birthday—the age she would formally become leader—Kasumi heard a noise coming from her brother's room. There, a ninja clad in black was about to slit Hayate's throat.

Noticing Kasumi, the assassin leaped out of the room like the wind. Kasumi pursued him to the edge of the village, where he turned back, let out an eerie laugh, and took his own life with a dagger.

That night, Kasumi left the village without telling a soul. It was an act that violated the ultimate law of the clan, turning her into a *runaway ninja* (nukenin). Yet, she had to make this journey. To avenge her brother, and to uncover the truth...

*"Brother, I'm on my way."* Kasumi rushed into the fog to hunt down his enemy.

### Character Traits & Playstyle

**Flexible movements, skillfully delivering kick techniques.**
It feels like Kasumi has received the most development care up to this point; her movements display a level of polish that sets her apart from the rest of the cast. In particular, her move where she quickly dashes into the opponent, grapples them, delivers a sudden elbow strike, and slips past to the other side looks beautiful—especially with the camera angle framing her finishing pose. It's a quintessential kunoichi technique.

Looking at her command list, she boasts a rich variety of leg techniques, ranging from crouching sweeps to lunging command throws reminiscent of a *Frankensteiner*. Kasumi also appears in the demo scenes performing her martial arts katas, which look incredibly cool!

By the way, though we can't show a picture of it here, her Player 2 color outfit is a light pastel blue. Which one you prefer is entirely up to you.

## Page 3: Developer Interview & "Morphing Technology"

### Title

**New Title Scramble! Must-read for anyone wanting to become a game creator! We ask the developers of DEAD OR ALIVE: "How a character like Kasumi is made."**

### Developer Comments (Tecmo 3D Section Chief)

> "People often ask me, 'A girl as the protagonist? A female ninja? Are you sure about that?' But I tell them it's great. It's totally fine. This girl is our resident idol, Kasumi-chan!
> At the AOU Show in Makuhari, thanks to a massive bug caused by one of our rookie programmers, her breasts ended up jiggling and bouncing around in a bizarre, exaggerated way. It was a delightful distraction that made it hard to look away, but it actually drew a ton of attention to the game. Kasumi-chan seems to have gained some strange confidence from it.
> Since then, our programmers won't stop talking about chest physics this or 'XX' that, and that's all they seem to work on. I'm over here telling them to forget about that for a second and fix the character model clipping during high-velocity motions!
> Jokes aside, Kasumi's development continues late into the night. She is still evolving and will become an even cuter character as we go. We can't let her lose out to Lei Fang, our Tai Chi user. We look forward to the day you all get to play the game. Jiggle jiggle!"

### The Mystery of "3D Morphing Technology"

**Breasts... bust... chest... There are many names for this symbol of femininity. Men admire it, and sometimes find themselves captivated by it. Indeed, while the level of fixation varies, it's safe to say that almost no healthy young man is completely indifferent to it.**

To realistically represent this on screen, developers use a method called **"3D Morphing Technology."** Here is what you need to know:

> "Ever since the AOU Show, a wild rumor has been circulating in the industry that Tecmo attached motion-capture sensors directly to a woman's breasts. Sadly, that rumor is completely false!
> Morphing is a technology used to deform digital objects, but at our studio, we mostly just use it on the girls. The engine calculates in real-time how the shape of the chest deforms based on the movement of the arms and waist during action sequences. It even simulates softness and elasticity. Naturally, a 17-year-old's physics (Kasumi) will behave differently than a 19-year-old's physics (Lei Fang). The entire staff spends every single day researching these nuances."
