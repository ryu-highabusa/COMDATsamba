_DESCRIPTION:	P1A_Character P1_CurrentCharacter
_VALUE TYPE:	Byte
_VALUE EXPLANATION:
Allows you to manipulate character selection and unlock hidden characters. P1A and P2A are for Solo (1vs1), P1A, P2A, P1B and P2B are for Tag (2vs2). The difference between Character and CurrentCharacter is that CurrentCharacter changes during the course of a match if you're playing Tag.
